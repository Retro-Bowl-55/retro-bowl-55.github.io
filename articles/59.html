How to approach Cut the Rope

Look at the candy, Om Nom, and every rope attached to the candy. Leaving one rope intact can let the candy swing past a star before you release it. Once all ropes are cut, you have much less control over where it falls.

Try the route to Om Nom first to learn the timing. On a second attempt, add a star to that route by cutting a little earlier or later. Keep the cut that lets the candy reach its target consistently.
