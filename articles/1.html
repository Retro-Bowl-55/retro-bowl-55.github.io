How to approach 1

Slide tiles with an arrow key or WASD. Equal values combine into half their starting value, so a smaller tile is the one to preserve. Choose a corner for that tile and bring matching numbers toward it.

Watch the empty spaces next to the smallest tile. Keep room to bring in another tile of the same value, and avoid a slide that scatters a pair across opposite edges unless it immediately creates space.
