How to approach Football Brawl

Stay goalside when the ball is bouncing and jump after you see its direction. Winning the first touch can send it straight at the other goal if your player is facing the right way.

Close to the opponent's goal, shoot when the keeper is moving away from the open side. If the ball breaks loose, recover toward your goal before trying to win another challenge.
