How to approach Skibidi Strike

Use the movement keys to keep a clear line of retreat in each game encounter. Click to fire, use the aim input when there's time for a steady shot, and step away from the action before pressing R to reload.

Save the T slow-motion input for a stretch where several enemies appear together and clearing space is urgent. Use the scroll wheel to find a workable weapon during a pause rather than cycling while enemies are already within reach.
