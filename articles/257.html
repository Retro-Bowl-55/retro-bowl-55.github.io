How to approach Tube Jumpers

Watch the angle of your float as waves pass under it. A short jump can restore your balance when one side lifts, but landing too close to an edge can make the next wave knock you off.

Try to stay near the center of your float while the other players jostle around you. Wait out a moving hazard when it will pass safely on its own, and jump only once you've identified where you'll land.
