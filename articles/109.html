How to approach Go Ball

Plan the route from the ball to the goal opening, leaving enough slope for gravity to keep it moving. A steep approach can send it past the turn you need.

If it bounces out near the finish, keep the opening route and flatten the last section. A gentler entry is easier to control than a sharp turn.
