How to approach Ballistic

Drag to aim and release to launch the entire ball volley. A rebound along a wall can send balls behind a row of high-value blocks, giving them several chances to hit instead of bouncing straight back.

Prioritize blocks near the bottom before collecting more balls, then look for white pickups on the same line as your bounce. An extra ball helps future rounds as long as the current shot still buys breathing room.
