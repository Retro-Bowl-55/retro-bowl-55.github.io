How to approach BitLife

Set a goal for the character before advancing the age button. A plan to get a particular career should guide education and activities; a relationship goal calls for time with people already in the character's life.

Check health, finances and relationships after an event before aging again. Deal with the meter most likely to cause a lasting problem while choices are still available, then see what the next year offers.
