How to approach Slope

Read the next segment of track while the ball crosses the current one. When you see a narrow entry, start lining up on the wider approach. A large adjustment at the seam can carry the ball off the far edge before you have a chance to correct it.

Use the red obstacles as landmarks for picking a route. Aim toward the center of the open side early, then release your steering input as you pass the block. At higher speeds, short corrections give you more chances to adjust without overshooting.
