How to approach Groovy Ski

A click changes the skier's direction. Watch the end of the current lane and change course early enough to be lined up before reaching the next obstacle. Keep the path short in crowded sections so there is time to change direction again.

If a collectible lies on the wrong side of a hazard, check where the return turn would happen before pursuing it. You may only have enough time to go out there or line up with the next opening. Choose the opening when the stretch ahead is hard to read.
