How to approach The Heist

Steer the getaway van through traffic while keeping an eye on the mission clock. Look for open lanes ahead and change over early; the most direct lane is rarely worth a collision that takes away speed.

Use the payout to address why the last mission failed. If the van took too much damage, strengthen it before buying more speed; if a mission calls for a specialist, check that requirement when deciding where to spend first.
