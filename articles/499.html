How to approach Gladihoppers

Movement and attack use different sets of keys. Practice moving into range with one hand and selecting an attack direction with the other. A swing started too far away leaves time for the opponent to close the gap and answer.

Test each stance in a quiet opening and see which attack reaches the area you intend to cover. Change stance when there is space to do it. When both fighters are close, focus on reaching a safer spot or making one well-timed attack.
