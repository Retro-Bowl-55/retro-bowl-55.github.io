How to approach Doodle Jump

The character bounces automatically, so your main job is to aim the descent. Steer only as far sideways as needed to reach a platform. A platform near the middle often gives you more routes upward than a bounce on the far edge.

Look at the next landing as you rise. If a platform moves, aim where it will be when you fall. A spring can take you higher quickly, so use the extra time in the air to identify a safe landing instead of drifting across the screen.
