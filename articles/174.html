How to approach PolyBranch

Watch branches farther down the low-poly corridor, where openings are easier to compare before they spread across the screen. Shift toward open space while you still have room to make a gentle correction.

If several branches overlap in perspective, focus on the nearest one and pick a route that still gives you a move afterward. A gap that looks widest on screen is less useful if it leaves you boxed in by the next branch.
