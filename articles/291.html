How to approach Adventure with Anxiety

Read each scene from the point of view of the anxious inner voice. The choices change how the conversation develops; pay attention to what the human fears before selecting a line.

On another playthrough, change an early response and keep reading long enough to see how the later scene shifts. This is a short story about anxiety, so exploring a different tone can be more rewarding than rushing to the end.
