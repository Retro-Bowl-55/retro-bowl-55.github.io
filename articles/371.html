How to approach Endless Truck

Read the active missions before you leave the start, then watch for fuel pickups as you drive. Use Space to jump over obstacles and use brief left or right inputs to set the truck's pitch before landing.

If a mission calls for flips, start the rotation early on a high jump. Level the wheels before touchdown and keep enough control to collect the next fuel can instead of spending the whole run on one stunt.
