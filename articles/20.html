How to approach Backrooms

Nearly identical rooms make it easy to walk in circles. When passing a memorable junction or an unusual feature, use it as a landmark and remember which direction you took from it. A short branch you can retrace is easier to explore than several unfamiliar turns in a row.

When the game gives you a clue about an exit, stop and connect it to a place you have seen. Return to the last recognizable junction if a corridor leads nowhere. A repeatable route leaves you more attention for changes in the surroundings.
