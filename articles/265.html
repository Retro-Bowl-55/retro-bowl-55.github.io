How to approach Vex 6

Work out a safe route through the stage before diverting for coins. A coin beside a narrow landing is easier to collect on a retry when you already know how to resume the route afterward.

Watch for where a narrow passage opens after a slide. Allow the stick figure to clear the ceiling before you jump, and choose a landing that gives you room to dodge the next hazard.
