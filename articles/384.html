How to approach G-Switch 3

Pay attention to which path is closing off and how long your runner will take to cross to the other surface. The safe side may change twice in a short stretch. Aim to finish the first flip early enough to set up the next one.

When a section gives you more than one runner to track, look for the one closest to an obstacle. Time the next flip against that runner, then check the other path as soon as the crossing begins. Find a landmark you can use on later attempts.
