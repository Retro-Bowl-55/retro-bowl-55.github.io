How to approach Matrix Rampage

Use the building's floors to put space between you and the fighters approaching. When one floor is crowded, move to an open part of the building and deal with enemies as they reach you. Check where you can go next before jumping into a busy room.

Pick up a nearby weapon when the path to it is open. Testing a new item in a small fight makes it easier to see its effective range. If the enemy count keeps building on one floor, leave before trying to collect everything on it.
