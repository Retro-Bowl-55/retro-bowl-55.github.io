How to approach Stickman Golf

Check elevation and hazards before choosing power. If a direct shot would give you a bad lie, aim for a flat spot with an open approach to the hole.

On the last stroke, use only the force needed to reach the cup. Change the landing point if the ball repeatedly rolls away on the same slope.
