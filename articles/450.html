How to approach Wordle Unlimited

Use consecutive rounds to compare opening guesses. Track how many distinct useful letters you uncover in the first two guesses, then decide which opening consistently leaves you with fewer candidates.

Watch for repeated letters when most positions are known. Feedback on one occurrence does not always tell you whether the answer can contain another in a different slot.
