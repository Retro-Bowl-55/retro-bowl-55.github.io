How to approach Pokemon Emerald

Use wild encounters near an early town to give more than your starter some experience. A Water, Grass, or Fighting move gives you another way to handle Roxanne's Rock team, especially once she sends out Nosepass.

Use the Poké Center before testing a longer route and inspect the bag for healing items you really use. Later in Hoenn, plan a team slot for travel moves such as Surf so the party can reach the next town without filling the same fighter's move list.
