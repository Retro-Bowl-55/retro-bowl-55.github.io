How to approach Avalanche

Blocks drop into the playfield and form a staircase as they settle. Let a falling block reach the floor before counting on it as a step. Stay near a lower ledge so you can climb without waiting for one large block to land in just the right place.

Aim for the side of the pile with the best route upward. A tall column can gain height quickly, but a sequence of shorter steps gives you places to move if another block falls over your head. Leave a gap to dodge sideways while choosing the next jump.
