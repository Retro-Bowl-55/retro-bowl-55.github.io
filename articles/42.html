How to approach Cannon Basketball 4

Use the first shot to learn the angle the ball takes away from the cannon. Change one part of the shot at a time. Raising the aim by a little helps you distinguish a shot that hits a ledge from one that clears it.

Look for anything that blocks the hoop and see if a shot can operate a switch along the way. When a straight route is closed, plan for a bounce off a nearby surface and watch where the ball lands on your first attempt.
