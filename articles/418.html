How to approach Pizza Tower

Build speed along open stretches and ease off before you reach a wall whose route you haven't seen. The momentum that gets Peppino across a gap can also carry him straight past a turn or an item you meant to grab.

When you trigger the escape, use landmarks from the outward trip to find the exit. Save risky detours for later attempts. Remembering where to let go of a dash often saves more time on the way back than trying to sprint through every room.
