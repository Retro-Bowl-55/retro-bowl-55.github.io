How to approach Box Head

Fight from a spot with room behind you. A corridor can concentrate zombies into one group, but it becomes dangerous if the zombies reach you from the other end. Move out into the open before both paths close.

When the game gives you another weapon, try it against a small group before relying on it in a crowd. Keep an escape lane clear while you turn to fire. If you keep getting crowded while attacking, stop chasing the last few enemies and reset your position first.
