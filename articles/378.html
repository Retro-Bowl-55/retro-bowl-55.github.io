How to approach Get On Top

Use WASD or the arrow keys to balance, press and pull in the grapple. The point goes to the player who forces the other's head to touch the floor, so use the body angle to decide which way to push.

A sudden opposite input can catch an opponent who is leaning into you. Experiment with a short crouch and lift on successive rounds instead of holding one direction for the whole struggle.
