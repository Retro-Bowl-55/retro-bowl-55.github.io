How to approach OvO 2

Read the route from the first ledge to the next broad landing. A small obstacle right after a gap can change how much forward movement you want to carry into the jump. Take a slower entry until you know where the safe landing starts.

If you come to a narrow passage, use the room before it to learn which movement input gets you through without clipping the edge. Mark the point where you need to jump again once you exit. Repeating those two cues makes it easier to put the section together.
