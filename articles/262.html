How to approach Vex 3

Treat each checkpoint as a chance to learn the next obstacle. Watch a moving blade complete a cycle, then decide where to stand and when to start. If a death sends you back, repeat the same setup and change the timing on your next attempt.

Read the landing as closely as the jump. Spikes beyond a ledge can punish holding forward after you clear a gap. For a low passage, approach with enough room to slide and watch where you emerge before committing to the next jump.
