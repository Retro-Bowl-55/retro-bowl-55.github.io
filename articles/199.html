How to approach Scrap Metal 3

Give the car room to build speed across the uneven ground before attempting a jump. Pick a flat landing area while approaching the ramp so the car doesn't come down onto a hidden slope.

If you use the object placement tools to set up a stunt, test one ramp first and watch where the car comes down. Adjust the gap between objects while keeping the car and approach speed the same.
