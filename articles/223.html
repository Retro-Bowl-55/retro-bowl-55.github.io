How to approach Station 141

Explore the station one room at a time. Pause at a threshold long enough to locate exits and enemies before moving into an open area, where discovering a route during a fight is harder.

Use calm stretches between encounters to check your weapon and revisit nearby supplies if needed. If a room repeatedly ends the attempt, enter from the doorway that gives you the clearest sight of the first enemy and a clear way back.
