How to approach 2048

Choose a corner for your largest tile and try to keep the next largest tiles along the same edge. This gives the small tiles somewhere to combine. Before using a direction that pulls your largest tile away, check whether another move can open a square while keeping your setup.

Make space as soon as the board begins to crowd. Small merges away from your main row can create room to prepare the larger merge you want. Look for a pair that will still line up after the board shifts, since a new tile can occupy an empty square after each move.
