How to approach Dino Bros

Before moving toward a coin, check what the same direction will do to both dinos. If one is near water, an otherwise safe move for its partner may end the attempt.

Use the terrain to change how far apart the dinos stand. When a wall holds one in place, the other may be able to take another step. Collect the difficult coins while that alignment is useful, then check the route to the exit for both.
