How to approach Super Mario Wonder

When a Wonder Flower is within reach, activate it from a safe position and pay attention to what changes in the course. Some altered sections reward exploring, while others immediately ask you to move along with the new terrain.

Equip a badge that addresses the part of the course you're struggling with when the option is available. For an awkward jump over moving ground, a recovery or movement badge can be more helpful than one aimed at collecting.
