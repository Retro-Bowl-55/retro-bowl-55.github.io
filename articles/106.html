How to approach Getaway Shootout

Use a few small jumps to learn how the character leans before letting go. The goal is to get to the escape point, so jump toward a landing that puts you ahead even if a rival is still fighting elsewhere. Keep climbing when there is a clear path.

Use a pickup to interrupt a rival only when the detour keeps you on the route. On narrow platforms, aim at getting both feet back on a safe surface. A big leap can cost the race if it sends you down to a section you have to climb again.
