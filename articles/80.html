How to approach Edge Let's Surf

Choose a path with clear water well ahead of the surfer. Steer early around rocks and other hazards so a sudden turn doesn't send you into a second obstacle right next to the first.

If something chases you after you wander into its area, move back into open water and keep going. Plan each pickup from the lane you can reach safely rather than making a hard sideways move at the last second.
