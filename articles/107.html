How to approach Gimme the Airpod

Keep the AirPod in view along with your opponent. Try moving to an open side of the play area before making your approach. That gives you more room to change course if the other player reaches the item at the same time.

Look at where the game leaves both players after a scramble. If your opponent is closer, avoid taking exactly the same approach again. Give yourself a route that can reach the item from a different direction and stay near enough to react when it moves.
