How to approach Among Us

This catalog's fan game puts you in the impostor role against bot crewmates. Watch the direction their patrols take and move through quieter areas while you look for a crewmate away from the others.

Use the on-screen ability buttons only when you're in position and have room to leave afterward. If the bots gather along a busy route, choose a different path instead of forcing another encounter in the same room.
