How to approach Blumgi Slime

Vary the jump charge to match the width of the gap. Practice with one short hop and one longer hop from the same starting point to learn how much charge you need.

Look for enough room to settle after landing. In two-player mode, each player has a separate jump key, so agree on who goes first when the landing space is tight.
