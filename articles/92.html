How to approach Fireboy and Watergirl

Check which character can cross each pool before moving. Fireboy can travel through lava and Watergirl can cross water. Keep both away from green hazards. When the routes split, give each character a safe waiting spot so you can focus on one crossing at a time.

Look for the relationship between gates, buttons and platforms. One character may need to stay on a switch while the other passes through. Plan how the first character will follow before moving them off the switch, and check that both can reach their matching exit doors.
