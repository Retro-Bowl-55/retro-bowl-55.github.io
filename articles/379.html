How to approach Gobdun

Before stepping forward in the dungeon, read what is directly in front of you. The forward command also attacks when an enemy blocks the way. If you are facing an open side passage and need to fight, turn toward the enemy before using that command.

Use the backward or block action according to the immediate situation. In a narrow passage, check where you can turn and whether you have a route back to a known room. When you reach a junction, choose a direction deliberately so you can retrace it if needed.
