How to approach Creative Kill Chamber

Read each scene as a point-and-click puzzle. Identify the exit and the character or device keeping you from it. Try the parts of the screen the game highlights, then watch what changed and whether that revealed a new interaction.

If a choice fails, repeat the opening clicks you know worked and change one decision near the failure. The level behaves more like a short sequence of events than a fight you can win by clicking faster. Remember where an item appeared so you can use it when needed.
