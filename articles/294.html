How to approach Basket Random

Watch the ball and the players' orientation at the start of each round. A jump only helps if your arms reach the ball while it is still in range.

When the conditions change, use the first exchange to learn the new bounce. If the ball is already headed toward your basket, focus the next jump on contesting it rather than trying to shoot immediately.
