How to approach Papery Planes

Guide the paper airplane through openings by looking ahead of its nose. Judge which obstacle will reach you first, then move toward the gap early enough to be settled when you arrive.

Take coins that follow your safe route. If a coin pulls you outward just before the next rock, pass it up and stay in a position from which you can dodge to either side.
