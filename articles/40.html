How to approach Big Tower Tiny Square

Use checkpoints to break the climb into small sections. On a ledge above hazards, line up with the exact landing area before jumping and keep movement short enough to stop on it.

If a section has a moving hazard, watch where it passes as you begin the jump. Change the launch moment before increasing speed, so the square crosses when the opening is available.
