How to approach Wheely 4

Point and Click Strategy · Practice notes for Wheely 4

When an early move leaves you with two choices, try to work out which one you can undo. Save choices that are hard to reverse until you have more information.

If you reach a dead end, identify the last choice that still gave you a viable route. Replay up to that decision and try the other route.
