How to approach Little Alchemy 2

Drag one element over another to experiment with a recipe. A new discovery often opens a whole branch: combine it with two or three familiar ingredients before moving on, rather than testing random pairs from the entire library.

Think about what a target does as well as what it's made of. If you want a transport item, try an existing vehicle with the place it moves through. Keep track of which new ingredients you have already tested so the expanding collection stays manageable.
