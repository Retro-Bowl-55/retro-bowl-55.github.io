How to approach Down the Hill

Read two steps of the descent at a time. When a branch leads toward a tree, spike or lava, take the other side while you can. Keep track of where that move will leave you so you have a choice at the following fork.

Look beyond a stationary obstacle for anything that might move into its lane. Cars and other moving objects can turn an apparent opening into a collision. Use the first few rows to settle the rhythm of left and right inputs before the hazard density rises.
