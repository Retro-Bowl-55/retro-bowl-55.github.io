How to approach Rooftop Snipers

Stay far enough from the edge that you can recover after a hit. Pay attention to the direction of your next jump, because a clean shot can be wasted if your own momentum takes you off the roof.

Watch the arc of your character's weapon before releasing a shot. When opponents jump straight toward you, let the descent bring them through your aim. Constant jumping makes both your landing and the shot timing harder to control.
