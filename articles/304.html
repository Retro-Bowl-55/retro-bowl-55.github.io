How to approach Iron Snout

Watch both directions for incoming wolves. Attack when the closest enemy comes within reach and turn before you press again if the other side is closing. Staying on one enemy too long can leave you open on the opposite side.

Adjust your movement to the height of the incoming attack. An enemy above you calls for a different response than one at ground level. If a dropped object comes within reach, decide whether you have time to grab it before the next wolf arrives.
