How to approach Papa's Cupcakeria

Match the batter and liner to the order before baking. Once both cupcakes are ready, compare the ticket's frosting and toppings for each one.

Use the center of the cupcake to start your frosting. Give yourself time to place the finishing decorations before taking on a new ticket.
