How to approach Moving Truck

Check how the load shifts before using Space for nitro. The side arrow keys tilt the truck, while up and down let you ease across a crest instead of sending the cargo high into the air.

If you lose the load on a drop, approach the edge more slowly and keep the vehicle aligned through the first bounce. Save nitro for a smooth stretch after you've regained control, or restart with R and compare the new approach.
