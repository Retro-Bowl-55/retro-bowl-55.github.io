How to approach Knife Master

Wait for a clear opening on the moving target before making the throw. Judge where the opening will be when the knife gets there, since the target can keep moving after you click. A shot that looks clear at release can be blocked by something already on the target.

Watch for changes in the motion after a hit. When the target spins at a different speed, rebuild your timing instead of throwing in the same rhythm. Use the wider gaps first so the last few throws have a predictable pattern to work with.
