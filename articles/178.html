How to approach Protektor

Escort the VIP to each floor's exit. Move only as far as you can safely support the person following you, and check where they are before rounding the next corner. Staying close enough to respond matters more than sprinting to the exit yourself.

Watch the ammunition supply. Protektor requires you to stand still to reload, so use a quiet stretch of the floor for that pause. If you take a detour for a star or pickup, check whether it leaves the VIP exposed.
