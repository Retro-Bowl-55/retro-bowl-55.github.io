How to approach Moto X3M 2

Read the slope on the far side of a jump before trying a rotation. Give the bike enough speed to clear the obstacle, then line up the wheels with the landing. Holding the same tilt all the way down can undo an otherwise clean jump.

For a faster finish, look at the obstacle after each landing. A slightly slower jump that lets you accelerate straight into the next segment can save more time than extra airtime. Practice one trouble spot at a time and note the approach that gives a repeatable landing.
