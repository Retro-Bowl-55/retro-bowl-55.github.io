How to approach Skywire

Watch the obstacle's full travel before moving the cable car into its lane. Back up if a moving object reaches the line while you're still too close to get through. You can wait on an open stretch before trying again.

Some obstacles have room between them and others have to be approached as a pair. If there is nowhere safe to stop after the first one, wait until the timing lets the cable car travel through both in one move.
