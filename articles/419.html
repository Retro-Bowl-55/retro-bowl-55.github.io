How to approach Pou

Start by checking Pou's needs. Feeding, cleaning and rest handle different gauges, so use the corresponding room when one is low. Keep some in-game coins on hand to cover everyday care.

Play the minigames when you need money for food or customization. Before spending on a new outfit, check the food supply. If Pou has just eaten, switch to another activity rather than spending coins on items you don't need yet.
