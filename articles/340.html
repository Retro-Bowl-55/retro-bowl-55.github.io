How to approach 1010 Deluxe

Completing a row or column removes its tiles, so a placement at their intersection can set up two clears. Look for a crossing where both lines are almost full before you spend a small piece on a spot away from either line.

Survey all the shapes you have available. When one piece is long or unusually wide, reserve its landing space first. Use the easier shapes to complete lines around it, especially if the clear would make a second location available.
