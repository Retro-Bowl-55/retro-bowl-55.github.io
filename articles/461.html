How to approach Learn To Fly 2

Learn To Fly 2 has you launch a penguin-shaped test dummy, then steer it through the air. Put together a balanced sled, glider, and propulsion setup before chasing a single expensive part. Let the ramp build speed and aim for a shallow glide once you're airborne.

For a distance attempt, use short bursts when speed begins falling. For a destruction attempt, save enough thrust to steer into the target with speed. Check the results screen to see whether your current setup loses more performance at launch or during flight.
