How to approach Boxing Random

Watch the position of each boxer before the first punch. The arena and physics can change after a point, so take a moment to see how far a jump will carry you in the new matchup.

Time a punch for the moment your glove is lined up with the opponent's head. If you are already leaning away, let the body swing back first or use the extra room to avoid giving up the next hit.
