How to approach Pacman

Use the outer paths to build a route through the dots, and leave at least one way out when you enter the middle of the maze. You can press the next arrow slightly ahead of a corner so Pac-Man turns as soon as he reaches it.

Save a nearby power pellet for when ghosts are actually closing in. If you take it too early, you may spend most of the blue timer crossing empty space. Resume clearing dots while the ghosts retreat so you have fewer side passages to revisit.
