How to approach Cartoon Mini Racing

Drive with the arrow keys and line the car up while the next turn is still ahead of you. Let off the accelerator when you can't see the exit so a sharp corner doesn't carry you off the course.

H resets a stuck car. Use it when the wheels no longer have a clear path back to the track, but try a short reverse first if you can recover without losing the position you've gained.
