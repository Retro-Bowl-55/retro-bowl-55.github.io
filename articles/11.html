How to approach Adrenaline Challenge

Motorcycle · Practice notes for Adrenaline Challenge

Take the opening section at a speed you can control. Watch how the vehicle responds to a small steering adjustment before trying a sharp turn.

If the same obstacle stops several runs, change the approach speed while keeping the same route. That helps you see whether speed is causing the problem.
