How to approach Subway Surfers New York

Move to the next subway lane as soon as you can see the track ahead open up. Use up for a jump and down for a roll, and keep the three lanes in view instead of following coins into a train you saw too late.

Activate your hoverboard with Space before a tight cluster of obstacles to give yourself room for an error. When your score is the priority, check missions before starting so you know which pickups can help your multiplier.
