How to approach 1 on 1 Soccer

Move with the arrow keys or use WASD in the two-player setup. Stay between your goal and the ball after a challenge so a high bounce doesn't give your opponent an open net. Use small steps to line up your next touch.

With a lead, make the opponent come to you instead of jumping at every ball. To break a tie late, approach from the side and try to direct the rebound behind the defender rather than sending it straight into them.
