How to approach Death Run 3D

Track the gaps in the moving tunnel walls and choose which side you need before the wall reaches you. Look for clusters that will leave only one safe route. Early steering leaves time for a small correction as that opening grows on the screen.

Practice a pattern for openings that switch sides. As soon as you clear a narrowing, move your eyes forward and start lining up the next gap. Avoid holding your direction after the view is aligned because the next opening may be on the opposite side.
