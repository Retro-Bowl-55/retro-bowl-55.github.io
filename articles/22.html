How to approach Bad Ice Cream 2

Leave yourself a second exit before clearing fruit in narrow parts of the maze. A wall across one entrance buys time only if you can still leave through the other side. Break your own ice if it is the fastest way out.

When playing with a partner, split fruit groups across different routes in the maze. Avoid placing ice right in front of each other, particularly when your partner needs that corridor to escape an enemy.
