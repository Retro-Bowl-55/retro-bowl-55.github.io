How to approach Basketball Stars

Sprint when you have an open lane, and use a pump fake if the defender keeps jumping to block. A possession can turn quickly, so look for an uncontested shot before using a Super on a crowded attempt.

On defense, get between the scorer and the hoop so a jump or block matters. Go for a steal when the opponent dribbles into range; a missed steal leaves them enough room for a quick shot.
