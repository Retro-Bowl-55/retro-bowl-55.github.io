How to approach Duck Life 2

Check how far your duck can keep up across changes in terrain. Climbing adds another skill to prepare, so train it when route previews or a failed race show a high obstacle is what cost you time.

Use coins for food before paying for optional changes to your duck's appearance when you have a race ahead. After a narrow loss, match your next practice session to the part of the course where you watched the lead disappear.
