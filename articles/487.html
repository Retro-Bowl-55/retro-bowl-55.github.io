How to approach Burnin' Rubber 5 XS

Use the arrow keys to drive and Z or X to fire your equipped weapons. Fire when you have a clear shot down a straight or when a rival slows through the same bend, so your attention stays on the course.

When you come up behind another car, leave enough space to avoid a collision if they brake. Passing on a clear stretch keeps your speed and leaves the next weapon ready for a real opening.
