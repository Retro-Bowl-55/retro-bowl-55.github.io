How to approach Core Ball

Look for an empty arc on the spinning center and fire your ball so it reaches the center as that arc passes. Account for the travel time from the launch point. Pressing just when the gap lines up may already be too late.

As the circle fills, wait for a gap with space on both sides instead of trying to squeeze past a peg. If a stage changes the rotation speed, watch a full turn before firing the last few shots.
