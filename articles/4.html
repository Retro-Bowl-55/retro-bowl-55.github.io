How to approach 10 Minutes Till Dawn

Move around the edge of the approaching group so the creatures stay on one side of you. Leave an avenue of escape before reloading, especially when several enemies are closing in from different directions.

Collect experience when you can cross the area safely. If a cluster is still crowded, circle back after drawing the enemies away. Choose upgrades that help with the problem that ended your previous run.
