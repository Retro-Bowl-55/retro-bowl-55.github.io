How to approach Wood Block Puzzle

Look at all available pieces before placing the first. Preserve a large patch for broad shapes and use smaller ones to fill gaps near the edges.

Clear a row or column when it creates space for the next group. Avoid cutting the middle into pockets that the larger pieces cannot fit.
