How to approach Generic Fishing Game

Space handles most fishing actions. Start by waiting for the cue that a fish is on the line, then follow the current prompt before pressing again. If a catch escapes, note which prompt changed first and use that as your cue on the next cast.

Open the Fishing Encyclopedia with P and browse with the movement keys to see which entries your catches have filled. Check it after catching something unfamiliar and then return to the lake. Discovering another species gives you a different goal from simply increasing the size of your catch.
