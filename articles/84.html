How to approach Endless War 3

Read the objective of each mission before moving your character. Mark the position you need to reach and the walls that interrupt the enemy's line of sight. Moving between two nearby cover points is easier to control than running the whole length of an open lane.

Watch when an enemy round passes your position. Use the break to reach your next spot and check how far it leaves you from the objective. If a route repeatedly stalls at a doorway, come back through a different opening on the map.
