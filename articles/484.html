How to approach Bob the Robber 4

Read the layout around the room you intend to enter. A patrol passing the doorway can make a quick entry safe and trap you on the way back. Stop in a quiet spot and watch a full cycle before deciding which doorway to use.

Separate progress toward the mission target from optional detours. Figure out how to reach the next required room first, then collect extras that you can grab on that route. If you discover another way back, use it to avoid crossing a watched doorway twice.
