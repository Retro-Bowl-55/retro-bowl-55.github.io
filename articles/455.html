How to approach Fancy Pants Adventures 2

Use the ramps to carry speed into the next jump. Hold on to that momentum when the ground ahead is clear, then ease off when the route folds back on itself. Look for a broad landing before trying a jump from a downhill run.

Optional shell challenges need a different pace from the trip through a level. Move the shell toward the hole in short, deliberate hits and watch how slopes affect it. If the shell rolls past your line, get ahead of it before sending it back.
