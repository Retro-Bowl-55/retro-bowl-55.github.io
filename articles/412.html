How to approach Big Shot Boxing

Use a jab to see how your opponent reacts, then follow with a stronger punch after you find an opening. If the other boxer is already guarding, backing out a step can reset the exchange without exposing you to an immediate counter.

Save room to retreat rather than pressing forward with every attack. After a blocked hit, raise your guard or step back. Come forward again when you see the opponent commit to a punch and leave their side open.
