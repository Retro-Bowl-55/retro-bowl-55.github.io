How to approach Apple Shooter

Each successful shot moves the bow farther from the game's apple target. The initial rounds are a chance to learn how the charge affects the on-screen trajectory. Compare the path of the arrow with where you were aiming so you have a reference for the next round.

Adjust one part of your mouse input after each attempt. Changing both the aim and charge at once makes an error harder to diagnose. As the range changes, remember that the previous shot gives you a useful starting point rather than an exact solution.
