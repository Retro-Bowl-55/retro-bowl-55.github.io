How to approach Rabbit Samurai

Grab a surface beyond a gap and let the rope swing the rabbit toward the landing. Lower anchors can help you pass an obstacle without gaining too much height.

Plan an anchor for the return trip before swinging toward an optional carrot. Use it to get back to the main route after collecting the pickup.
