How to approach Jungle TD

Tower Defense · Practice notes for Jungle TD

After a failed wave, compare where the first enemy escaped with where most of your damage was dealt. That shows which part of the route to reinforce.

If enemies slip through one side, spend your next upgrade where it can affect that route. Keep an eye on the remaining budget before committing.
