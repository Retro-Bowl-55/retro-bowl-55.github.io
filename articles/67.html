How to approach Defend the Tank

Move defenders around the tank according to where the pressure is building. A defender on the quiet side contributes little when attackers have stacked up at the opposite end. Shift protection before the tank reaches a critical stretch.

Check the tank during any lull in the wave. Use repair opportunities when both sides are relatively clear. If you move your whole defense at once while enemies approach, you can leave the tank exposed, so give one position coverage while adjusting the other.
