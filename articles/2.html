How to approach 1v1.LOL

Practice building one piece of cover and switching back to your weapon before trying a long build sequence. Place cover when you need time to reset your aim, then move to a different edge before taking another shot.

Use the training obstacles to isolate movement and weapon switching. Try the same route twice and see whether you can finish it without looking down at the keys when you swap from materials to a weapon.
